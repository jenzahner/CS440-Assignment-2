Fashion-MNIST, Han Xiao, Kashif Rasul and Roland Vollgraf, Zalando Research (2017).
Original source: https://github.com/zalandoresearch/fashion-mnist
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.07747
Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/zalando-research/fashionmnist
License: MIT; see FASHION-MNIST-LICENSE.txt.

This assignment uses the original 60,000 training images, split into 54,000
training and 6,000 validation images (600 validation images per class; seed 440).
The loader flattens the 28 x 28 images and divides pixel values by 255.
The official 10,000 test images are reserved for evaluation and are not in this archive.
